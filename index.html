Our Story

A home on the highway, the Rajasthani way

At the edge of the highway at Pallu, where the desert road runs towards Hanumangarh, stands Shri Maha Maya — a family-run hotel and restaurant built on one simple promise: treat every guest like a guest of the house.

Our kitchen is 100% pure vegetarian — no eggs, no compromises. Flour from the local mill, desi ghee, spices ground by hand. From a full thali to soft phulkas, kadhi and seasonal greens, every plate tastes like it was cooked for family — because it is.

Atithi Devo Bhava — the guest is God — is not a slogan on our wall; it is the way we run every room, every table and every early-morning chai.

A promise to every guest

Spotless rooms, honest prices and pure-veg food cooked fresh — whether you arrive at noon or midnight.
